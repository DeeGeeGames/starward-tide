How to play

Tap a blue world, then any destination to send. Tap the source again to deselect. Use Multi-select to gather sources, then turn it off and choose a destination. You can also send to friendly worlds.

Troops capture surfaces. Ships win orbit. Guns defend but never travel. A dashed enemy-colored ring means blockade: the surface cannot dispatch. Tap a blue orbital ring over an enemy world to redirect your ships.

Enemy guns and ships stop landings. Transports wait in orbit and can be destroyed; escorts absorb fire first. Take every rival planet to win. Switching production discards unfinished work.
